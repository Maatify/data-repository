# Contributor Code of Conduct

This project and all contributors are expected to uphold high standards of professionalism, respect, and collaboration.  
The following code of conduct is adapted from the **Contributor Covenant v2.1**, widely used across the open-source community.

---

## 📌 Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of **maatify/data-repository** pledge to:

- Foster a welcoming and inclusive environment
- Act respectfully toward all contributors
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, or toxic behavior
- Encourage constructive participation
- Maintain a project culture based on mutual trust and professionalism

Our goal is to create a pleasant and productive space for everyone working on the Maatify ecosystem.

---

## 🤝 Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior:

- Being respectful in communication and collaboration
- Providing constructive feedback
- Accepting different viewpoints
- Helping others learn and grow
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and apologizing when needed

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment or insults
- Discriminatory language or imagery
- Threatening or violent behavior
- Personal attacks or trolling
- Sharing private information without permission
- Disruptive or hostile conduct

---

## 🛡️ Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct.

They have the right to:

- Warn contributors
- Reject or revert contributions
- Ban individuals from the project
- Remove inappropriate content or comments

Enforcement decisions must be fair, transparent, and consistent.

---

## 🚨 Reporting Misconduct

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it privately to the Maatify team:

📧 **security@maatify.dev**

Reports are handled confidentially.

---

## 🛠 Enforcement Process

1. Report is reviewed by maintainers
2. Maintainers may request more information
3. Action is taken depending on severity
4. Reporter receives follow-up confirmation
5. Incident is documented internally

We aim to respond within **48 hours**.

---

## 🌍 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- The project repository (issues, PRs, discussions)
- Maatify.dev communication channels
- Any public or private space involving project collaboration
